# Choose which stations to service

One truck leaves the depot with bikes and may visit any subset of the stations in `/root/work/data.json`. Choose its route to minimize travel distance plus the penalty for unserved rebalancing targets. The answer depends on both the station coordinates and their targets.

The input contains `depot` and `stations` coordinates as latitude and longitude in degrees, `vehicle_capacity`, `start_load`, and `penalty_weight`. Each station has a nonconsecutive integer `id`, `initial_bikes`, `station_capacity`, and a signed `net_rebalancing_target`. A positive target means picking up that many bikes; a negative target means dropping off its absolute value. If visited, a station's entire target must be performed. An unvisited station incurs `penalty_weight * abs(net_rebalancing_target)`. The truck may visit each station at most once. Its load must remain between zero and `vehicle_capacity` after every visit, and every resulting station inventory must remain within its capacity. An empty route is permitted.

Compute great-circle distances in miles using Earth radius 3960.0; do not round individual legs. Write `/root/work/route.json` with exactly `route`, `travel_distance_miles`, `unmet_penalty`, and `objective`. The route starts with `"depot_start"`, ends with `"depot_end"`, and contains visited station IDs in order. Round the three numeric fields to six decimal places. Among equal-objective routes within 1e-9, choose the lexicographically smallest sequence of station IDs.
