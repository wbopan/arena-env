# Spectral measurements around phase windows

`/root/work` contains four `station_*.npz` records and `windows.csv`. Each record contains `data`, a samples-by-three float array; `channels`, comma-separated channel names in column order; and `dt`, its sampling interval in seconds. Channels ending in `Z` are vertical; those ending in `N` or `E` are horizontal. `windows.csv` contains `file_name,phase,start_idx,end_idx`. Its P and S windows are half-open sample intervals: include `start_idx` and exclude `end_idx`.

Produce three measurements per record. For the P window, compute the one-sided real FFT of the vertical samples and square the magnitude of each coefficient. `p_high_fraction` is the sum of powers at frequencies at least 8 Hz divided by total power. For the S window, perform the same calculation separately on both horizontal channels, add their powers at each frequency, and report `s_high_fraction` using the same 8 Hz boundary. Include the zero-frequency coefficient in total power. Do not detrend, taper, or normalise the samples before either FFT.

Also calculate `s_hv_ratio`: the sum of squared samples on both horizontal channels in the S window divided by the sum of squared vertical samples there. Write only `/root/work/spectra.xlsx`, with one worksheet named `Spectra`. Its first row must be `file_name,p_high_fraction,s_high_fraction,s_hv_ratio`; subsequent rows are sorted by filename. Store numeric measurements rounded to four decimal places.
