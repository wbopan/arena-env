Two compact FMCW radar captures, `/root/work/sweep_01.bin` and `/root/work/sweep_02.bin`, each have a matching JSON sidecar. These are real-only, little-endian int16 ADC samples. The sidecars specify samples per chirp, chirp rate, chirp count, carrier frequency, and metres per range bin. Consecutive groups of samples form successive chirps; there is no I/Q interleaving in this format.

Produce a range-gated radial-displacement trace for each capture. Subtract each chirp's mean ADC value and apply a real FFT along its fast-time samples. At each range bin, subtract the complex mean across chirps to remove stationary clutter. Among bins whose nominal ranges are from 0.3 through 0.9 metres, inclusive, select the bin having the largest mean magnitude after clutter removal; ties go to the lower bin. Unwrap that bin's phase across chirps, then subtract its ordinary-least-squares best-fit line against chirp time. Convert residual phase to millimetres with `phase_rad * (299792458 / carrier_frequency_hz) * 1000 / (4*pi)`.

Write exactly `/root/work/gated_motion.csv`. Its header must be `capture_id,second,displacement_mm`. For each capture in numeric order, report the displacement at chirp indices `0, chirp_rate_hz, 2*chirp_rate_hz, ...` up to but not including the chirp count. `second` is the integer index divided by chirp rate. Round displacement to three decimal places. Do not report the selected bin or any intermediate FFT data.
