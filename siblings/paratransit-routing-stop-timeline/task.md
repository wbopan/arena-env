# Reconstruct a vehicle stop timeline

A dispatcher has fixed the order of one vehicle’s stops. Reconstruct its operating timeline from the inputs in `/root/work`: `requests.json` contains trip IDs, pickup and dropoff node IDs, passenger loads, and inclusive service-start windows in minutes after midnight; `route.json` contains `start_min` and the ordered `node_sequence`, including depot `DEP` at both ends; and `t_matrix.csv` is a headerless integer travel-time matrix. Its row and column order is `DEP,P0,D0,P1,D1,...`, following trip order in `requests.json`.

Start the vehicle at the first depot at `start_min`. For each subsequent stop, arrival equals the preceding departure plus matrix travel time. At a service stop, start service at the later of arrival and its window’s lower bound; departure is service start plus its service duration. At the final depot, service duration and waiting are zero. Pickup adds its trip’s load, and dropoff subtracts it. The first depot has load zero. All times are integer minutes after midnight.

Write `/root/work/timeline.csv` with exactly these columns, in this order: `node,arrival_min,start_min,depart_min,wait_min,load_after`. Include one row per node occurrence, including both depots. Use integers, with no rounding. The supplied route respects all window upper bounds and a vehicle capacity of four; your timeline must preserve that feasibility.
